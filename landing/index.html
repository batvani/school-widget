Tapping any School Widget opens a full-screen menu. Try the tabs on the phone.

  • TodayEvery class with its time and room. After the last bell, the next school day.
  • TimetableThe whole week at once, with tests and important classes in red.
  • Days offHolidays, exams and your own days off, and how many school days are left.
  • SettingsColours, language, calendars, reminders and updates.

Every day off, in your calendar too.

School Widget downloads the official holidays and exam days for your grade by itself. Add them to Apple, Google or Outlook Calendar as a subscription: it updates itself and moves up a grade every September.

Install in 2 minutes.

You need an iPhone, the free app Scriptable and the School Widget Installer Shortcut.

Your timetable is ready

Install first, then tap Open in School Widget. Or copy it and tap Paste in School Widget.

Step 1 of 3

1

Get Scriptable

Scriptable is a free app that runs School Widget. Install it and open it once.

Open the App Store
2

Add the Shortcut

School Widget Installer opens in Shortcuts. Tap Add Shortcut.

Add the Shortcut
3

Run it

It downloads School Widget into Scriptable and opens it. Tap Allow the first time.

Then add the widget. Press and hold your Home Screen, tap +, choose Scriptable and a size. Tap the new widget, set Script to School Widget, and you are done.

The Shortcut does not work?

Copy the installer script and run it in Scriptable. Or open the code and paste it into a new Scriptable script by hand.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. School Widget is open source (MIT) and Scriptable is a free app.

What does it send, and where?

Nothing about you. Your timetable stays on your phone. The widget only downloads updates and the holiday list from GitHub, and nothing you type on this page is sent anywhere.

The widget does not change

iOS decides when a widget refreshes. Open School Widget once, or press and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget and pick the script again.

Can I use it in Bulgarian?

Yes. Bulgarian is the default language and English is one setting.
